# Privacy notice template – legal review required

ReviewPulse AI processes merchant account data, review content, and only the minimum customer identifiers needed for review management, escalation, and data-rights handling. Review content is stored in the merchant's configured EU database region and a redacted subset may be sent to OpenAI for reply generation. The app does not install storefront tracking, pixels, or fingerprints.

This is a technical draft, not legal advice. Confirm controller/processor roles, international transfer safeguards, retention periods, contact details, and the final list of subprocessors with counsel before publication.
