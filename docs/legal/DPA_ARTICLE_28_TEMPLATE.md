# Data Processing Agreement outline – legal review required

Subject matter: review ingestion, safety classification, AI draft generation, approval and provider publishing.  
Data subjects: merchant users and customers whose review identifiers are supplied by the merchant.  
Categories: shop/session data, review text/rating, minimum customer identifiers, usage/audit data.  
Subprocessors: Shopify, Supabase (Frankfurt), OpenAI, hosting/CDN, and transactional email only when actually used.  
Controls: tenant authorization, encryption at rest/in transit, PII minimization, access logging, incident process, deletion/export workflows, and key rotation.

Legal counsel must complete the instructions, duration, transfer mechanism, audit language, technical measures, and annexes.
