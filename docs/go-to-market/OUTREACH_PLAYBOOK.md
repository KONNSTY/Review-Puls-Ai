# Zero-ad-spend outreach playbook

1. Build a consent-respecting list of 50 candidate stores from public Shopify App Store/merchant directories; do not scrape private data or bypass access controls.
2. Qualify only public signals: active store, visible review volume, and a small sample of unanswered reviews.
3. Personalize one observation and offer a no-pressure 10-minute walkthrough; never claim the app replied to a real customer unless it did.
4. Record opt-out requests and do not follow up after an opt-out.
5. Measure contacted → replied → demo → trial activated → first approved reply → paid conversion.

Subject: A calmer way to keep up with {{store}} reviews

Hi {{first_name}}, I noticed {{specific public observation}}. ReviewPulse AI helps small Shopify teams draft replies in their own voice, while legal/safety-risk content stays human-reviewed.
