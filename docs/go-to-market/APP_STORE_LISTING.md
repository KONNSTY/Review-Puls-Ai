# Shopify App Store listing draft

## Title

ReviewPulse AI – Review Reply Manager

## Subtitle

Turn customer reviews into thoughtful replies in minutes.

## Value proposition

ReviewPulse AI brings reviews into one focused workspace, flags risky conversations, and creates concise drafts in your brand voice. Approve safe replies with one click while critical content stays human-reviewed.

## Features

- Review queue with status, rating, provider, and date filters
- Brand voice and company values
- PII scrubbing before AI processing
- Safety and legal-risk escalation
- CSV launch importer with row-level validation
- Opt-in rating-gated auto-reply
- Usage visibility and Shopify-native billing path

## FAQ

**Does it publish without approval?** Only when the merchant explicitly enables auto-reply and the review passes every rating and safety gate. **What happens to risky reviews?** They are escalated and blocked from auto-publish. **Does it slow my storefront?** No storefront scripts or theme assets are installed. **How do I start?** Connect a supported provider when verified, or upload the documented CSV template.
